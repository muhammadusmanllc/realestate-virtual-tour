Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience

Home / Cookie Policy
Last updated: October 8, 2024

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better browsing experience by remembering your preferences, analyzing website performance, and personalizing content.

Why We Use Cookies

Cookies enable us to enhance your experience by providing personalized property recommendations, remembering your search preferences, and improving our website's functionality.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly:

  • Website navigation and security
  • Form submissions and user sessions
  • Shopping cart and saved searches
  • Authentication and account access

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors interact with our website:

  • Page views and user behavior
  • Popular content and features
  • Website performance metrics
  • Error tracking and optimization

Preference Cookies

Remember your choices to personalize your experience:

  • Search filters and sorting preferences
  • Language and location settings
  • Display preferences and themes
  • Notification and communication choices

Marketing Cookies

Deliver relevant advertisements and content:

  • Personalized property recommendations
  • Relevant advertising content
  • Social media integration
  • Email marketing optimization

3. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party services that may set their own cookies:

Google Analytics

Provides detailed insights into website usage and visitor behavior to help us improve our services.

Google Privacy Policy

Google Maps

Powers our interactive maps and location services for property searches and neighborhood information.

Social Media

Social sharing buttons and embedded content from platforms like Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn.

6. Your Consent

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner. You can:

  • Accept All: Allow all types of cookies for the full experience
  • Customize: Choose which categories of cookies to allow
  • Essential Only: Allow only necessary cookies for basic functionality
  • Change Preferences: Update your choices at any time through our cookie settings

Manage Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in our website footer or contacting us directly.

7. Children's Privacy

Our real estate services are designed for adults. We do not knowingly collect cookies or personal information from children under 16. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us immediately.
