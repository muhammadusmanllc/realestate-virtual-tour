Get Your Finances Ready

Strong financial preparation is the foundation of successful home buying

Determine Your Budget

Consider these costs:

  • Down Payment: Typically 10-20% of home price
  • Monthly Payments: Principal, interest, taxes, insurance
  • Closing Costs: 2-5% of home price
  • Moving Expenses: $1,000-$5,000+
  • Emergency Fund: 3-6 months of expenses
Rule of Thumb: Your monthly housing costs should not exceed 28% of your gross monthly income.

Improve Your Credit Score

Boost your score by:

  • Pay all bills on time
  • Reduce credit card balances
  • Don't open new credit accounts
  • Keep old accounts open
  • Check credit reports for errors
740+ Excellent
680-739 Good
620-679 Fair

Gather Required Documents

Mortgage application documents:

  • Pay stubs (last 2 months)
  • Tax returns (last 2 years)
  • Bank statements (last 3 months)
  • Employment verification letter
  • Investment account statements
  • Proof of other income sources

Evaluating Properties Like a Pro

Know what to look for when viewing homes

Exterior Inspection

Roof & Gutters

  • Missing or damaged shingles
  • Sagging roofline
  • Clogged or damaged gutters
  • Water damage signs

Foundation & Structure

  • Cracks in foundation
  • Settling or shifting signs
  • Water pooling around foundation
  • Proper drainage

Siding & Windows

  • Condition of exterior siding
  • Window functionality
  • Caulking and weather stripping
  • Paint condition

Interior Inspection

Electrical System

  • Electrical panel condition
  • Outlet functionality
  • Light switch operations
  • GFCI outlets in wet areas

Plumbing System

  • Water pressure test
  • Drainage efficiency
  • Visible pipe condition
  • Water heater age/condition

HVAC System

  • Heating and cooling operation
  • Filter condition
  • Ductwork inspection
  • Energy efficiency ratings

Major Red Flags to Watch For

Foundation cracks or settling
Electrical system issues
Plumbing problems
Roof damage or leaks
Mold or water damage
Pest infestations

  • Time on Market: How long it's been listed
  • Seller Motivation: Reason for selling

Buyer's Market Strategy

Understanding Contingencies

Inspection Contingency

Allows you to have the home professionally inspected and negotiate repairs or walk away if major issues are found.

Typically 7-14 days

Financing Contingency

Protects you if you can't secure financing. Your earnest money is returned if the loan falls through.

Usually 30-45 days

Appraisal Contingency

Ensures the home appraises for at least the loan amount. Protects you from overpaying.

Typically 2-3 weeks

Sale of Current Home

Contingent on selling your current home first. Makes offers less attractive but necessary for some buyers.

Varies widely

First-Time Buyer Special Considerations

Extra tips for those buying their first home

First-Time Buyer Programs

FHA Loans

Down payments as low as 3.5% with more flexible credit requirements

  • Lower credit score requirements (580+)
  • Mortgage insurance required
  • Loan limits vary by area

VA Loans

No down payment required for eligible veterans and service members

  • No mortgage insurance
  • Competitive interest rates
  • No prepayment penalties

USDA Loans

Zero down payment for eligible rural and suburban properties

  • Income restrictions apply
  • Property must be in eligible area
  • Below-market interest rates

State/Local Programs

Down payment assistance and favorable loan terms

  • Grants for down payment/closing costs
  • Below-market interest rates
  • Income and location restrictions

Avoid These First-Timer Mistakes

Skipping Pre-approval

Get pre-approved before house hunting to understand your budget and show sellers you're serious.

Maxing Out Your Budget

Leave room for unexpected expenses, maintenance, and lifestyle costs beyond the mortgage.

Skipping the Inspection

Always get a professional inspection, even in competitive markets. It could save you thousands.

Focusing Only on Monthly Payment

Consider total cost including taxes, insurance, PMI, HOA fees, and maintenance costs.

Emotional Decision Making

Stay objective and don't fall in love with a house before understanding all the costs and risks.

Rushing the Process

Take time to research, compare options, and make informed decisions. Good homes will come along.

The Closing Process

  • Review all closing documents
  • Arrange moving logistics

  • Conduct final walkthrough

  • Title insurance
  • Attorney fees
  • Recording fees

Prepaid Items

  • Property taxes
  • Homeowners insurance
  • Mortgage interest
  • HOA fees (if applicable)
