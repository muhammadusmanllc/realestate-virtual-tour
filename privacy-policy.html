Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information with complete transparency

Home / Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 8, 2024

1. Introduction

At RealEstate Pro, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our services, or interact with us in any way.

We believe in complete transparency about our data practices. This policy covers all our real estate services, including property listings, virtual tours, consultation services, and any other interactions you may have with RealEstate Pro.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

Information you voluntarily provide to us:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Property preferences and search criteria
  • Financial information (for pre-qualification)
  • Communication preferences
  • Property viewing appointments

Technical Information

Information automatically collected:

  • IP address and location data
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information and screen resolution
  • Pages viewed and time spent
  • Referring websites and search terms

Property Information

Real estate related data:

  • Property search history
  • Saved properties and favorites
  • Virtual tour interactions
  • Property inquiry details
  • Market analysis requests

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide exceptional real estate services and improve your experience:

Primary Uses

  • Service Delivery: Provide property search, virtual tours, and consultation services
  • Communication: Send property updates, market insights, and respond to inquiries
  • Personalization: Customize property recommendations based on your preferences
  • Transaction Support: Facilitate property viewings, negotiations, and closings
  • Legal Compliance: Meet regulatory requirements and industry standards

4. How We Share Your Information

We respect your privacy and only share your information in specific circumstances:

Authorized Sharing

  • Real Estate Partners: Licensed agents, lenders, inspectors, and other professionals involved in your transaction
  • Service Providers: Technology partners, marketing platforms, and administrative services
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or regulatory authorities
  • Business Transfers: In connection with mergers, acquisitions, or asset sales (with notice)

Your Control

We never sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes. You can opt out of marketing communications at any time.

5. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your personal information:

Technical Safeguards

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • Secure database storage with encryption
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Firewall and intrusion detection systems

Administrative Controls

  • Limited access on need-to-know basis
  • Employee training on data protection
  • Confidentiality agreements
  • Regular access reviews and updates

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have important rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we have about you
  • Correction: Ask us to update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable, machine-readable format
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your information

Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@realestatepro.com or call +1 (555) 123-4567. We'll respond within 30 days.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law:

  • Active Clients: Throughout our business relationship and for 7 years after completion
  • Website Visitors: Analytics data for 2 years, contact forms for 3 years
  • Marketing Lists: Until you unsubscribe or request deletion
  • Legal Requirements: As mandated by real estate regulations and tax laws

9. Third-Party Services

Our website integrates with trusted third-party services to enhance functionality:

  • Google Analytics: Website performance analysis
  • Google Maps: Property location services
  • Email Services: Communication and marketing platforms
  • Payment Processors: Secure transaction processing
  • Virtual Tour Providers: 3D property visualization

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. When we make significant changes:

  • We'll update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • We'll notify active clients via email
  • We'll post notices on our website
  • We'll obtain consent if required by law
